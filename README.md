## 🎯 Project Overview

An interactive, single-page Excel dashboard that turns raw e-commerce sales data into clear business insights. It helps a manager answer, at a glance: *How much are we selling? How profitable is it? Where, through which channel, and which products drive the revenue?*

All figures are in USD, for the year 2026 (Jan–Dec).

## 🔑 Key Results (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | **$645,223** |
| Gross Profit | **$296,384** (~45.9% gross margin) |
| Avg. Price | **$238.35** |
| Units Sold | **6,698** |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Strong profitability:** Gross profit is about 46% of revenue, and it moves in line with revenue every month.
- **Seasonality:** Revenue peaks in **June (~$70K)**, with a second spike in **July and November (~$66K each)**. The lowest month is **February (~$36K)**.
- **Product categories:** **Camping ($198.5K)** and **Hiking ($180.0K)** together bring in about **59%** of total revenue. Travel ($123.7K), Cycling ($87.5K) and Fitness ($55.6K) follow.
- **Sales channels:** **Online Store is the biggest channel at 40%**, then Corporate (28%), Retail Partners (18%) and Marketplace (14%).
- **Geography:** **United Kingdom** is the top revenue country, followed by strong European markets such as Germany and Spain.
- **Bestsellers (units):** Trail Boots (449), Summit Backpack 45L (447), Compact Sleeping Bag (433), Hiking Daypack 20L (396), Trail Bike Light (392).

## 🧩 Dashboard Features

- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Avg. Price, Units Sold
- **Revenue & Gross Profit trend:** monthly line chart with data labels
- **Revenue by Country:** bar chart across European markets
- **Revenue by Channel:** donut chart (Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, Retail Partners)
- **Revenue by Product Category:** ranked bar chart
- **Top 5 Bestsellers:** products ranked by units sold
- **Interactive filters (Slicers + Timeline):** Order Date (by quarter), Customer Type (Business / New / Returning), Region (Central, Northern, Southern, Western Europe), Sales Channel. Every chart and KPI updates instantly when a filter is clicked.
- **Navigation buttons:** Analyze, Data, Contact for quick movement between sheets

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open `Sales Excel Dashboard Shruti.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
2. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet.
3. Click any slicer button (Customer Type, Region, Sales Channel) or the Order Date timeline to filter the whole dashboard.
4. Use the filter-clear icon at the top-right of each slicer to reset.
